# 2026-11-12 ZB BTCUSDT intraday signal-timing audit

## Scope and provenance
- Source: 177 contiguous LBank native-1m-stream-derived 5-minute NATIVE_CANDLE_CLOSED runtime records, 2026-11-12 08:45–23:25 KST candle starts, close through 23:30.
- The received_at timestamps are Railway log observation timestamps. Source candle closure is stream-inferred, not independently LBank REST-verified.
- Dataset: lbank_native_5m_177bars.json in this folder. It does not include private accounts, orders, user messages or credentials.
- Distinguish real-time direction forecasts, actual order opportunity, and later known price trajectory. Post-event low cannot be treated as a target known when the original forecast was made.

## Observed deficiencies
1. Formal VIDEO_ALERT_DECISION: 55 no_qualified_entry decisions and one stale_or_before_start from 19:00–23:30 KST logs. Observational alerts were delivered independently. This does not establish profitable hypothetical trades.
2. Existing trend_priority previously promoted A-short when a brief 5m bounce rose above EMA9 even as completed 15m and 1h were bearish. In causal replay, four completed bars ranked A under the previous rule but B under the corrected rule: 21:15, 21:20, 21:50 and 21:55 KST bar close timestamps. Actual 12-bar resistance was not tested and rejected.
3. Existing evaluate_b_failed_reclaim required waiting 1–3 whole 5m bars after the initial break; it is not an initial-break evaluator. On 21:13–23:30 KST runtime logs, the independent B research lane recorded 18 no_recent_qualified_breakout, 5 retest_not_confirmed_or_chasing, 2 insufficient_net_rr, 3 support_reclaimed, 1 startup stale. This was not an entry lane.
4. Two counterfactual examples: Around 20:44 KST A-short observation level 73,450–73,500 was not revisited (max subsequent 5m high 73,131.5 through 23:30). At ~21:18 KST A-short observation 73,430–73,470 likewise was not revisited (subsequent max 73,113.7).
5. An early B-support-break concept must not be confused with a short sell limit placed below current price. A sell limit below market may execute immediately; a breakdown-triggered stop order has different activation/slippage behavior. If a sell limit is placed after price fell below it, it waits for a bounce. No trading order is generated by this patch.
6. A historically observed daily low far below current price is a remote scenario reference, not automatically a reliable target. Using it to manufacture cosmetically favorable 2:1 net RR can create false confidence.

## Changes
- Separate bearish higher-timeframe directional bias from current entry trigger. In bearish 15m/1h regime B-short priority persists through shallow EMA9 bounces. A-short only leads after an actual completed resistance test + bearish rejection + volume. Trend_priority returns entry_qualified false; rank is not a trade signal.
- Add first confirmed 1-minute support-break research path using LBank native 1m stream, pre-existing 6 completed 5m supports and earlier 1-minute volume. Detect first breach without waiting for three later 5m closes. Availability only after the 1-minute bar is closed.
- Before qualifying any paper entry require known lower structural target, stop and assumed net risk-reward >=2:1. Unknown targets, gaps, stale receipts and future 5m history are excluded. A future real-price executable check remains essential.
- Immutable per-minute journal. NO new Telegram recommendation, NO automatic order and NO modification to existing RSI/CCI entry policy.
- Add regression tests for resistance prioritization, first-minute detection, no incomplete candles, gaps, stale histories, no future-data use and idempotence.

## Limitations and activation gate
The 1-minute path has not been independently backtested with source-authentic LBank 1m receipts across up/down/range regimes. A small synthetic test or more observational alerts is not evidence of profitability. Do not promote it to live entry Telegram or orders until out-of-sample transaction replay establishes net outcomes, false signals, spreads/slippage, funding and liquidation exposure.
